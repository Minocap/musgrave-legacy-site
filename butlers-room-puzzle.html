The Under-Butler's Chamber

Move your cursor over the room to examine it closely. Three details have been marked for inspection. Click each one to record what you find, then submit your conclusion below.

THE TRUNK Beneath a folded coat, the lining has been recently unpicked and re-stitched with thread that doesn't match.
THE FLOORBOARD One plank sits slightly proud of its neighbours, its nail heads scratched as though lifted and replaced more than once.
THE DRAWER A small brass key, too fine for any lock in this room, is tucked beneath a folded handkerchief.

Taken together — what has the under-butler hidden, and where?
